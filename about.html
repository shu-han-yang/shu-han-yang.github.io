About

It all started with a simple idea —

Draw one small piece of art and share it each day(…almost), just to keep the habit going.

Mini Art A Day @miniartaday

Over time, these drawings became a growing library of hand-drawn illustrations.

This is Sunny Draws —

Here offering free hand-drawn little minimal illustrations.

Best for digital content (web, image or video) and small prints.

You can download and use the illustrations, modify them for your own projects, and use them for personal or commercial work.

Who is Sunny?

I am an animator working in Tokyo.

I built this website to share my art for people to use.

Even though AI can generate illustrations today, I still treasure the ability to create as a human being.

To me, SunnyDraws.com is not only an art library, but a place where I can leave something lasting behind—a little memento of my lifetime on Earth.

Hope you can find help here with my art :)
