About

It all started with a simple idea —

draw one small piece of art and share it each day(…almost), just to keep the habit going. (Mini Art A Day @miniartaday)
Over time, these drawings became a growing library of illustrations.

This is Sunny Draws —

offering free hand-drawn little minimal illustrations.

Best for digital content (web, image or video) and small prints.You can:

• download and use the illustrations
• modify them for your own projects
• use them for personal or commercial work
