About

It all started with a simple idea —

Draw one small piece of art and share it each day(…almost), just to keep the habit going.

(Mini Art A Day @miniartaday)

Over time, these drawings became a growing library of illustrations.

This is Sunny Draws —

Here offering free hand-drawn little minimal illustrations.

Best for digital content (web, image or video) and small prints.
